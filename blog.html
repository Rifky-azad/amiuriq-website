Who we are

Built by builders, driven by purpose

Amiuriq Pvt Ltd is a Sri Lanka-based software company delivering cutting-edge digital solutions to clients across the globe, making enterprise-grade technology accessible and impactful.

From our headquarters in Colombo, our distributed team of engineers, designers, data scientists and strategists collaborates across time zones, disciplines and borders to deliver software that truly moves the needle.

  • Innovation first. We invest in R&D so clients get solutions built on what works today and tomorrow.
  • Client-obsessed. Every decision passes one test: does this create real value for our clients?

Our environment

A workspace that energises

Where and how you work shapes what you build. Our environment is designed to spark creativity, remove friction and fuel deep focus.

Modern tech stack

Every team member gets premium hardware and licensed access to the best development tools, cloud platforms and productivity software, no excuses, just execution.

Open collaboration

A flat hierarchy means ideas flow freely. Junior developer or senior architect, your voice shapes product decisions through standups, design reviews and retros.

Continuous learning

We sponsor certifications, conferences and internal knowledge-sharing. Learning is built into how we operate, with dedicated growth time every sprint.

Wellbeing-centred

Healthy work rhythms, no-meeting focus blocks and team socials. Burnout is not a badge of honour, sustainable pace is how we build great things.

Diverse & inclusive

Our team spans multiple countries, backgrounds and disciplines. Diversity directly improves the quality of our thinking and our products.

Recognition & growth

Exceptional work is recognised publicly and rewarded meaningfully, with clear career ladders and merit-based growth.

IT-based employee support

Powered by smart IT systems

A robust internal IT infrastructure automates the mundane, resolves issues fast and keeps every team member fully equipped.

Device & equipment management

Hardware is centrally managed through MDM. New hires receive pre-configured workstations on day one, zero setup friction.

24/7 internal helpdesk

Our IT ticketing system responds within 2 hours and resolves 90% of issues the same day, from software bugs to VPN access.

Cloud access & licensing

Immediate access to AWS, GCP and Azure sandboxes plus JetBrains IDEs, Figma, Postman and all essential SaaS tools.

Security & access control

RBAC, SSO and mandatory MFA protect every company system, keeping team and client data safe without friction.

AI-assisted workflows

Internal AI tools automate boilerplate, summarise sprint notes and draft client updates, so the team focuses on high-value work.

Performance dashboards

Real-time dashboards give everyone visibility into project health, deployment pipelines and system uptime.
